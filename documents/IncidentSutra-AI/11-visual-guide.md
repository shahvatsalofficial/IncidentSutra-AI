## Four kinds of statement

Every sentence in a report belongs to exactly one of these.

<!-- svg:evidence -->

| Kind | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Observed | In an imported record, with an evidence ID | "checkout logged a timeout at 19:49 UTC" |
| Inferred | A hypothesis built from observations | "The release may be related to the errors" |
| Unknown | Data that was not supplied | "No metrics: impact cannot be measured" |
| Not run | A check nobody executed | "Compare error rate before and after the release" |
